Fork release — peak/valley badge

- Every column in the AI usage panel now shows a compact 42 x 19 peak/valley badge right after its name and plan chip.
- The badge carries a 24-hour colour band, a live "now" pointer, the current period and its multiplier, and changes colour between peak (orange), normal (blue-grey) and valley (teal).
- Hover shows the current rate, a second-accurate countdown and the next switch time. Click opens a card with the countdown, a 24-hour timeline and a legend.
- Data comes from the service's own `CreditHours` definition when available, otherwise from the user's own off-peak periods in Settings. With neither configured, an explicitly labelled example schedule is shown (weekdays 09-12 and 14-18 at 2x, the rest at 0.5x; weekends at 0.5x). No accounting uses it.
- The badge refreshes once a second and stops its timer when the column is rebuilt.
- This is a fork of v1.3.1. See FORK.md for the exact changes, build instructions and licence notes.

分支版本 — 峰谷图标

- AI 用量页每一列的名称与套餐徽章之后新增一枚 42 × 19 的峰谷图标。
- 图标含 24 小时色带、当前时刻指针、当前时段与倍率，并随高峰（橙）/ 平时（蓝灰）/ 低谷（青）换色。
- 悬停显示当前倍率、精确到秒的倒计时与下次切换时刻；点击展开完整卡片（倒计时、24 格时间轴、图例）。
- 优先读取服务商 `CreditHours` 定义，其次读取设置里的自定义低峰时段；两者都没有时显示已明确标注的示例时段（工作日 09–12、14–18 ×2，其余 ×0.5；周末全天 ×0.5），不参与任何实际计费。
- 本版本基于 v1.3.1 修改，改动清单、构建与许可说明见 FORK.md。
